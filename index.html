Warmth in the Winter Chill | 2026 Thanksgiving Message from Boya Professional Alliance (BPAUS)

The winter of 2026 feels especially brisk for international students and professionals building their lives in North America. Shifting macro winds and evolving visa pathways have made every step of putting down roots abroad harder than before. Yet the founding team of Boya Professional Alliance (BPAUS) and the thousands of peers beside us are precisely those who stand firm in the storm—anchoring their lives in knowledge, hard work, and resilience.

Having weathered similar headwinds ourselves, we deeply understand the grit and aspirations that bind our community together. Drawn from classical Han dynasty literature, the name "Boya" (博雅) embodies broad learning and upright character, resonating closely with the Western liberal arts tradition. Positioned at the forefront of business and technology in North America, we embrace a spirit of boundlessness—welcoming talent regardless of background or alma mater, united purely by public-spiritedness, genuine dedication, and shared ideals. As a true community of the younger generation, we value action and accountability over titles or seniority. Our core executive team is powered primarily by professionals from the 1980s through 2000s generations, who will also take the helm of our future Board of Directors. At the same time, our generous circle of sponsors spans across generations from the 1960s to the 2000s—from the financial backing of seasoned mentors to the wholehearted support of our peers, every tangible contribution is deeply cherished. We strive to build a bridge for cross-disciplinary exchange and collaboration—beginning with passion, guided by ideals, gathering bright minds, and walking forward in wisdom—so that young doers can grow through real-world leadership into the next generation of industry pioneers and civic leaders.

Since our official launch in late July 2026 and receiving IRS 501(c)(3) federal tax-exempt status in mid-October, BPAUS has delivered a vibrant and meaningful first chapter in less than four months:

Four months of intensive effort mark only a modest debut for Boya Professional Alliance—our journey has just begun. Ahead lies a broader stage of greater ambition and impact, reaching more fellow travelers and uniting an even stronger force for good.

From scattered sparks to a thriving community, every milestone carries the warmth of your support. This Thanksgiving, the Executive Team and Board of Directors of Boya Professional Alliance extend our deepest gratitude to everyone who has chosen to walk alongside us and light the way forward:

Thank you to our Founding Directors, whose foresight and sense of responsibility charted our course from day one and built this nonprofit spiritual home for international students, scholars, and professionals;

Thank you to our Executive Team led by Ms. Chengcheng Qin, whose tireless dedication and seamless execution over the past hundred-plus days brought every intellectual exchange and community gathering to life;

Thank you to every generous Sponsor, especially the many friends who stepped forward with financial support out of pure trust even before our 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status was officially approved in mid-October—your selfless backing gives us the confidence to move steadily forward amid uncertainty;

Thank you to every Speaker and Coach, who shared their insights, experience, and encouragement without reservation when our community needed direction and inspiration most;

And above all, thank you to the more than 600 participants and fellow travelers who showed up, spoke up, swung a racket, and shared candid conversations—proving that however long the road ahead, none of us walks alone.

Times and cycles may bring a passing chill, yet the kindness, professionalism, and sincerity of a united community are more than enough to warm the longest winter night. Thank you for making "Boya Professional Alliance" not merely a name, but a true harbor for us all.

Wishing you a joyful Thanksgiving! May warmth fill your hearts, light guide your steps, and peace stay with you through every season.

The Executive Team & Board of Directors, Boya Professional Alliance (BPAUS)

BOYA Roundtable (Sessions 1 & 2): Navigating Quantitative Finance in the AI Era

On November 6 and 12, 2026, Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) hosted the first two sessions of the BOYA Roundtable series in Midtown Manhattan, gathering over 70 quants, PMs, and executives from top buy-side firms, investment banks, and tech companies. Led by industry-veteran Featured Guests, the closed-door discussions explored AI’s impact on Pod vs. Central structures, real-world AI trading limits, and cultivating a personal research edge—fostering candid, cross-generational dialogue on talent and strategic leadership.
