01 / About Us

Our Mission & Charter

Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) is a New York-incorporated 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

Rooted in classical literature, the philosophy of “Boya” reflects intellectual breadth and moral distinction, finding harmony with the liberal arts ideals of the West.

Anchored at the intersection of commerce and technological innovation across North America, the Alliance serves as a collaborative hub for emerging leaders and high-caliber professionals across diverse sectors.

Founded on passion and inspired by purpose, BPAUS convenes exceptional talent to foster meaningful professional dialogue and interdisciplinary advancement.
