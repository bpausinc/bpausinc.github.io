01 / About Us

Our Mission & Charter

Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) is a New York-incorporated 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

Rooted in classical literature, the philosophy of “Boya” reflects intellectual breadth and moral distinction, finding harmony with the liberal arts ideals of the West.

Anchored at the intersection of commerce and technological innovation across North America, the Alliance serves as a collaborative hub for emerging leaders and high-caliber professionals across diverse sectors.

Founded on passion and inspired by purpose, BPAUS convenes exceptional talent to foster meaningful professional dialogue and interdisciplinary advancement.

BOYA Roundtable (Sessions 1 & 2): Navigating Quantitative Finance in the AI Era

On November 6 and 12, 2026, Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) hosted the first two sessions of the BOYA Roundtable series in Midtown Manhattan, gathering over 70 quants, PMs, and executives from top buy-side firms, investment banks, and tech companies. Led by industry-veteran Featured Guests, the closed-door discussions explored AI’s impact on Pod vs. Central structures, real-world AI trading limits, and cultivating a personal research edge—fostering candid, cross-generational dialogue on talent and strategic leadership.

BOYA Career Accelerator Workshops (Issue 2): Making Your Capabilities Visible & Rewarded

On October 23, 2026, Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) hosted Issue 2 of the BOYA Career Accelerator Workshops in Tribeca, generously sponsored by Yuli Ma, Founder of FocusAlpha AI. Featuring bestselling author and data science executive Weihua Li, the interactive session explored sensory communication alignment, managing up, and high-impact 1-on-1s—equipping attendees with practical strategies to break through invisible career ceilings and ensure their work is recognized.

2nd Boya Tennis Fundamentals & Clinics

On August 30, 2026, Boya Professional Alliance Inc. (BPAUS) hosted the 2nd Boya Tennis Fundamentals and Clinics in Edgewater, NJ, gathering over 70 attendees for an afternoon of professional coaching, biomechanical instruction, and inclusive community athletics. The 501(c)(3) subsidized program fostered athletic literacy, court etiquette, and interdisciplinary networking outside corporate environments.
